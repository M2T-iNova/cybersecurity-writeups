# My Experience with Bug Bounty Hunting: How I Explain It to Others

*Understanding the value of independent vulnerability research and how to communicate practical experience beyond traditional employment.*

## About this article

This article explores my personal experience with bug bounty hunting, a journey I started in 2022 and have continued alongside my academic studies.

It reflects on the practical activities involved in independent vulnerability research, the tools and methodologies I have explored, the challenges encountered along the way, and how this experience can be communicated and valued in a professional context.

Rather than presenting independent research as a substitute for professional employment, the article examines its role in developing practical familiarity, technical curiosity, and independent problem-solving skills.
